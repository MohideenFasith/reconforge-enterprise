# Features

- Multi-tenant account and financial-entry management
- JSON and CSV ingestion
- Exact, tolerance, fuzzy, one-to-many, many-to-one, and net-group matching helpers
- Reconciliation runs with ambiguity protection
- Duplicate and suspicious-reversal detection
- Exception case lifecycle and review queues
- Adjustment workflow with material approval threshold
- Balanced journal validation and posting
- Period close readiness controls
- Business calendars, variance rules, materiality, FX conversion
- Exception aging, SLA state, risk and quality scoring
- Evidence attachments and dispute lifecycle
- Outbox, jobs, webhooks, audit trail, lineage, snapshots
- Deterministic exports and operational reporting
